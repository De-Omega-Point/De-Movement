Mobility priorities for your handstand

Wrists and fingers

Daily gentle movement. Count heavy planche, pressing and handstand sessions together for load management.

Shoulder elevation

Overhead reach, wall slides and controlled scapular movement.

Hip compression

90/90 transitions, seated pike and relaxed adductor work complement press preparation.

Recovery

Bedtime practice should leave you calmer, not exhausted. Gentle stretching supports range and comfort; claims of isolated fascia remodelling are unproven.

Safety: Stop poses causing sharp pain, tingling, numbness or increasing wrist symptoms. Adjust downward dog or cat–cow to forearms when wrists are irritated. Use professional assessment for persistent symptoms. Choose side-lying relaxation if lying flat is uncomfortable.
