12-week progression

Control before depth

  • Weeks 1–4: shallow holds and supported balance.
  • Weeks 5–8: longer holds and improved control.
  • Weeks 9–12: deeper comfortable ranges, less support.
Loading rule: Don't turn recovery yoga into another heavy knee session. Reduce holds after demanding squat, split-squat or jumping days.

Why these poses?

Muscles: quadriceps, glutes and calves control positions.

Tendons: static holds provide some load, but progressive resistance remains the main driver.

Fascia: movement loads the wider connective tissue system; claims of selective fascial “release” are unproven.

Mobility: practise useful ankle, hip and knee ranges without forcing end positions.

Stop sharp pain, locking, giving way or swelling. Seek clinical guidance if symptoms persist.
