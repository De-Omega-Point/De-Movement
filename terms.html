TERMS · PILOT

Move with judgement.

These pilot terms describe how the current De-Movement build should be used while it is being tested and improved.

Movement and health

De-Movement provides movement-training information, programming tools and self-reported readiness features. It is not medical diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation or emergency advice.

Do not use an app recommendation to override pain, injury, dizziness, unusual symptoms, professional advice or your own judgement. Stop an activity when continuing would be unsafe.

Human control

Passport progression is intentionally human-controlled. Readiness criteria and Assistant suggestions are decision support, not automatic clearance. Coaches remain responsible for their own professional judgement and scope of practice.

Accounts

Keep access to your sign-in email secure. Do not attempt to access another person’s data or bypass role controls. Accounts may be suspended where necessary to protect the pilot or other participants.

Local data

Local-first storage means some data may exist only on your browser or device. Browser resets, storage clearing or device loss can remove it. Export important local data when you need a portable copy.

Pilot availability

The pilot may change, be unavailable, contain defects or reset non-essential test data while the product is being developed. Features should not be relied on as the sole record for professional, clinical or legal purposes.

Age and pilot participation

The general pilot is intended for adults aged 18 and over unless De-Omega-Point runs a separately approved supervised program with appropriate consent and safeguards.

Content and software

De-Movement software, brand elements and original product content remain subject to their applicable ownership and licence terms. External references remain the property of their respective owners.

Before public launch

These are pilot terms, not the final commercial terms. Pricing, refunds, formal support, governing-law wording and any paid-service conditions should be finalised before broad public sale.
