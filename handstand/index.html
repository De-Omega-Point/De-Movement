Mobility finishers

Forearm flexor stretch: 2 × 20 seconds/side, arm straight, palm up, gently draw fingers back.

Puppy pose: 2 × 30 seconds, breathe and lower chest without forcing shoulders.

Pike stretch: 2 × 30 seconds, hinge forward from hips, no forced rounding.

Pancake: 2 × 30 seconds, sit wide, hinge forward gently.

Load management: Count planche and heavy pressing as part of your weekly wrist/shoulder load. Allow roughly 48 hours between demanding hand-loading days initially. Reduce load if you develop worsening or persistent symptoms; stop and seek assessment for sharp pain, numbness or tingling. Tendon adaptation takes time; fascia cannot be selectively lengthened with these drills.
