PRIVACY NOTICE · PILOT

Local first means local first.

De-Movement is designed so its core movement tools can work on your device without a cloud account. Cloud features are additive, not mandatory.

What stays on your device

Your local training profile, readiness settings, Movement Passport, saved Flow, training history and Assistant context are stored in browser storage when you use De-Movement locally.

Clearing browser/site data can remove that local copy. Use the data export feature before clearing your device if you want a backup.

When you connect an account

If you choose to sign in, selected De-Movement data can sync to the D-Move cloud backend so it can follow you across devices and support coaching features.

Synced data can include your profile, goals, readiness check-ins, Passport state, training logs, saved Flows and Coach assignments.

Coach access

A Coach can view movement evidence only for Movers in an active coaching relationship. Private Coach notes are not visible to Movers. Relationship access is enforced at the database layer using row-level security.

Assistant

The current De-Movement Assistant is local and evidence-bound. It uses your local profile, readiness, history and Passport to explain recommendations. Core Assistant use does not require an external language model.

If optional cloud AI is introduced later, it will require a separate privacy review and will not silently become the authority over Passport or training decisions.

Pilot feedback

Pilot feedback is submitted only when you press Send. De-Movement does not need hidden behavioural tracking to function. Submitted feedback may include a rating, product area, comment and build version.

Account and data controls

You can export your local De-Movement data. A connected account also provides an account deletion control. Deleting a cloud account removes the associated De-Movement profile and cloud-linked movement data through database cascades.

What we avoid

De-Movement is not designed to sell personal movement data, use training data for behavioural advertising, or silently convert a readiness note into a medical diagnosis.

Pilot scope

This notice describes the current pilot build. Before broad public release, De-Omega-Point should review retention periods, support/contact details, jurisdiction-specific privacy obligations and any future third-party AI or analytics providers.
